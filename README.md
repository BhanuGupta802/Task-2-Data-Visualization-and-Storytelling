Key insights in plain language
Key insights
Sales grew 51% from $484K (2014) to $733K (2017), with profit growing alongside it — but discounting is starting to eat into margin.
Furniture is a profitability problem. It generates 32% of total sales but only 6% of total profit. Tables alone lost $17,725, the single biggest drag on margin.
Discounting hurts profit disproportionately in Furniture. The Discount-vs-Profit scatter shows Furniture's trend line sloping sharply downward as discount increases, while Office Supplies and Technology tolerate discounts much better.
Some high-sales states are still losing money. Texas (-$25,729), Ohio (-$16,971), and Pennsylvania (-$15,560) are the three biggest loss states — worth investigating on pricing or shipping cost grounds.
